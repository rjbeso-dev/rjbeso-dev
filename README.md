### Current work

- **[freight-agent](https://github.com/rjbeso-dev/freight-agent)**: an AI agent that triages freight delivery exceptions. It reads the carrier notification, decides the remedy, acts, and escalates the calls too big or too uncertain to make alone. TypeScript on Claude Opus 4.8, with a 7-scenario evaluation harness it passes 26 of 26.
- **[operator-os](https://github.com/rjbeso-dev/operator-os)**: multi-pipeline AI operator system (n8n + Claude Haiku 4.5 + Notion). Inbox triage in about 50 seconds per 20 emails, plus a meeting processor fanning out to parallel Notion databases.
- **[studio-ops](https://github.com/rjbeso-dev/studio-ops)**: AI project-kickoff pipeline (n8n + Claude + Notion) that drafts kickoff briefs from discovery notes and flags notes too thin to brief, routing them to a human instead of inventing details.
- **[bible-buddy](https://github.com/rjbeso-dev/bible-buddy)**: a calm, editorial Bible reading and study web app with grouped translations, side-by-side compare, and private in-browser highlights and notes.
